# Campus Lunch

Build a simple, phone-readable campus lunch menu webpage.

Requirements:
- Show Monday's three meals initially.
- Each meal should show a name, description, price in KRW, and vegetarian label when applicable.
- Use a white background, black text, and thin gray meal cards.
- Add a labeled day selector with Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday.
- Use the sample meal data in menu-data.json.
- Changing the selected day updates the meal cards and the meal count.
- Keep the meal data in app.js.
- Do not use fetch or a server; the page must work by opening index.html directly.
- Keep index.html, style.css, and app.js at the project root.
- The page should be readable on a phone.
